Privacy Policy

NewLife · Effective 19 November 2026

✨The short version

No account Saves stay on your phone Ads by Google AdMob

NewLife never sends your game to a server. The ads come from Google, and Google may collect device data, such as your advertising ID, to show and measure them. You can control that at any time.

👋Who we are

NewLife is a life-simulation game made by an independent developer, Anubhav Dubey ("we", "us"). Android package: com.anubhavdubey.newlife.

Questions about privacy: programmingwithanubhav@gmail.com

📱What stays on your phone

The game saves your current life, past lives, unlocked looks and settings (sound, music) only on your device. We never receive this data.

  • We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos or microphone.
  • There's no sign-in and no account.
  • We don't use our own analytics or crash-reporting services.

📺Ads from Google AdMob

NewLife shows banner, full-screen and optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and (where you allow it) personalize them, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address and the approximate location it gives
  • device and app details (model, Android version, app version, language)
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, rewarded videos watched)

We don't see or control this data. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy. Google explains how it uses this data in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

🛡️Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalized. If you say no, you still see ads, but they aren't personalized.
  • US states with privacy laws (such as California): you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for ads in the same form.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & ad choices (shown where the law requires it).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (on some phones: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You can play the whole game without watching one.

📤Sharing your life story

When you tap Share at the end of a life, the game makes an image on your phone and opens Android's share menu. The image goes only to the app you choose, and that app's privacy policy applies.

🗑️Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings → Erase all data to delete every life on your phone. Uninstalling the app does the same. We keep no copy, so there's nothing to delete on our side.

To manage ad data held by Google, use the choices above or My Ad Center.

🧒Children

NewLife is made for players 13 and older and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content rated for teens. If you think a child under 13 is using the game, email us and we'll help.

🔒Security

Your saves sit in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Ad requests to Google use encrypted connections (HTTPS).

📝Changes to this policy

If NewLife starts handling data differently, for example by adding an in-app purchase, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
